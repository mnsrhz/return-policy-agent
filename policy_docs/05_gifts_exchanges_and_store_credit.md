# Northstar Commerce Gifts, Exchanges, And Store Credit Policy

## [POLICY:EXCHANGE_SIZE_COLOR_WINDOW] Size And Color Exchanges

Size and color exchanges are allowed within 30 days of delivery when the item is otherwise eligible for return or exchange. The item must meet the same condition, packaging, and proof requirements that apply to standard returns unless support approves another path.

Citation-friendly rule: Size and color exchanges are allowed within 30 days when the item is eligible.

Example: A customer who bought a shirt in medium may request a large within 30 days of delivery if the shirt is unused, unworn, unwashed, in original packaging, and supported by proof of purchase.

Agent handling rule: For exchange requests, check the 30-day delivery window, item eligibility, condition, packaging, and proof of purchase before giving a decision.

## [POLICY:EXCHANGE_INVENTORY_AVAILABILITY] Inventory Availability For Exchanges

Exchanges depend on inventory availability. Northstar Commerce cannot guarantee that a requested size, color, style, or variant will be available when the exchange is processed.

Citation-friendly rule: Exchanges depend on inventory availability.

Example: A customer may qualify for a size exchange, but the requested replacement size may be out of stock. In that case, support may offer a return, refund, or store credit option based on the applicable policy.

Agent handling rule: Never guarantee a replacement item. If the customer asks whether a specific size or color is available, explain that inventory must be checked by the store system or support.

## [POLICY:GIFT_RETURN_REQUIREMENTS] Gift Return Requirements

Gift returns require a gift receipt, order number, or other proof that the item was purchased from Northstar Commerce. Gift returns must also satisfy applicable return window, item condition, packaging, and restricted-item rules.

Citation-friendly rule: Gift returns require a gift receipt or order number.

Example: A customer returning a gifted scarf should provide the gift receipt or order number so Northstar Commerce can verify eligibility.

Agent handling rule: If the customer says the item was a gift and does not provide a gift receipt or order number, ask for one before making an eligibility decision.

## [POLICY:GIFT_REFUND_STORE_CREDIT] Gift Refund Method

Gift returns are usually refunded as Northstar Commerce store credit. Store credit may be issued to the gift recipient when support verifies the gift purchase and approves the return.

Citation-friendly rule: Gift returns are usually refunded as store credit.

Example: A gift recipient who returns an eligible item with a gift receipt may receive store credit rather than a refund to the purchaser's original payment method.

Agent handling rule: Explain that gift returns usually result in store credit, but do not promise store credit until eligibility and support approval are confirmed.

## [POLICY:STORE_CREDIT_USE] Store Credit Scope

Northstar Commerce store credit may be used for future Northstar Commerce purchases subject to the terms provided when the credit is issued. Store credit is not the same as a cash refund.

Citation-friendly rule: Store credit may be used for future Northstar Commerce purchases and is not a cash refund.

Example: A customer who receives store credit for a gift return can apply that credit to a future purchase from Northstar Commerce.

Agent handling rule: When explaining store credit, distinguish it from original-payment refunds and avoid promising transferability, cash redemption, or expiration terms unless those terms are present in the available policy documents.

## [POLICY:EXCHANGE_RESTRICTED_ITEMS] Exchange Restrictions

Items that are final sale, digital, downloadable, or personalized are not eligible for standard exchange unless a specific damage, defect, wrong item, incorrect customization, or not-as-described issue requires support review.

Citation-friendly rule: Restricted items are not eligible for standard exchange unless a support-review issue applies.

Example: A personalized hoodie cannot be exchanged for a different size because the customer chose the wrong size. If the hoodie arrived with the wrong customization, support review is required.

Agent handling rule: For exchange requests involving restricted items, apply restricted-item rules before standard exchange rules.
