# Northstar Commerce Refunds And Shipping Fees Policy

## [POLICY:REFUND_ORIGINAL_PAYMENT] Refund Method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Northstar Commerce does not issue cash refunds for purchases made by card, digital wallet, gift card, or store credit.

Citation-friendly rule: Approved refunds go to the original payment method.

Example: If a customer paid with a credit card, an approved refund is returned to that credit card. If a customer paid with store credit, an approved refund is returned as store credit.

Agent handling rule: Explain the refund method only after clarifying that refund approval depends on eligibility, inspection, and support review when applicable.

## [POLICY:REFUND_TIMING_AFTER_INSPECTION] Refund Timing

Approved refunds are processed 5 to 10 business days after Northstar Commerce receives and inspects the returned item. Bank, card issuer, and payment provider posting times may add additional time after Northstar Commerce processes the refund.

Citation-friendly rule: Refunds are processed 5 to 10 business days after inspection.

Example: If a returned item is inspected on Monday and approved, Northstar Commerce processes the refund within 5 to 10 business days after that inspection date.

Agent handling rule: Do not tell the customer a refund has been issued unless the system has that order status. In this project, the agent should describe policy timing only.

## [POLICY:INSPECTION_REQUIRED_BEFORE_REFUND] Inspection Before Refund Approval

Northstar Commerce may inspect returned items before approving a refund. Inspection may verify item identity, condition, packaging, accessories, damage claims, and whether the return matches the reason provided.

Citation-friendly rule: Returned items may require inspection before refund approval.

Example: A customer returns headphones for buyer's remorse. Northstar Commerce may inspect that the headphones are unused, unworn, unwashed if applicable, and include original packaging and accessories before approving a refund.

Agent handling rule: If the customer asks whether they will definitely receive a refund, explain that refund approval may depend on inspection.

## [POLICY:STORE_CREDIT_WITH_MISSING_PROOF] Store Credit When Proof Is Missing

When proof of purchase is missing, Northstar Commerce support may approve store credit instead of a refund to the original payment method. Store credit for missing-proof cases is discretionary and requires support approval.

Citation-friendly rule: Store credit may be issued when proof of purchase is missing but support approves.

Example: A customer without a receipt may be routed to support review. Support may approve store credit if the item can be verified and the return is otherwise acceptable.

Agent handling rule: Do not promise store credit. If proof is missing, explain that support review is required and store credit may be possible only if support approves.

## [POLICY:RETURN_SHIPPING_CUSTOMER_REASONS] Return Shipping For Customer-Initiated Reasons

Return shipping is not refunded for buyer's remorse, wrong size, changed mind, color preference, duplicate order, or ordered-by-mistake returns. These are customer-initiated reasons.

Citation-friendly rule: Return shipping is not refunded for buyer's remorse, wrong size, changed mind, or ordered by mistake.

Example: A customer returns a sweater because they ordered the wrong size. If the return is otherwise eligible, the original return shipping cost is not refunded under the customer-initiated reason rule.

Agent handling rule: If the reason is customer-initiated, cite this rule when explaining shipping fee responsibility.

## [POLICY:RETURN_SHIPPING_NORTHSTAR_REASONS] Return Shipping For Northstar Or Fulfillment Issues

Return shipping may be refunded when the return reason is damaged, defective, wrong item, missing item, or not as described. Support review may be required before approving the shipping-fee refund.

Citation-friendly rule: Return shipping may be refunded for damaged, defective, wrong, missing, or not-as-described items.

Example: A customer received the wrong color due to fulfillment error. Support may approve return shipping reimbursement after reviewing the order and issue evidence.

Agent handling rule: If the customer reports a Northstar or fulfillment issue, do not guarantee shipping reimbursement. Explain that return shipping may be refunded after support review.
