# Northstar Commerce Final Sale And Exceptions Policy

## [POLICY:FINAL_SALE_NO_RETURNS] Final Sale Items

Items marked final sale at the time of purchase cannot be returned or exchanged. Final sale status may appear on the product page, cart, checkout page, receipt, or order confirmation.

Citation-friendly rule: Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged.

Example: A clearance handbag marked "Final Sale" on the product page is not eligible for return or exchange because the final sale restriction was disclosed before purchase.

Agent handling rule: If the customer states the item was final sale, classify the case as ineligible unless the customer also reports damage, defect, wrong item, missing item, or another support-review issue.

## [POLICY:PERSONALIZED_CUSTOM_ITEMS] Personalized And Customized Items

Personalized, monogrammed, engraved, altered, made-to-order, or customized items cannot be returned or exchanged unless the item arrived damaged, defective, incorrect, or not as described by Northstar Commerce.

Citation-friendly rule: Personalized and customized items are not returnable unless damaged, defective, incorrect, or not as described.

Example: A necklace engraved with the customer's initials cannot be returned because the customer changed their mind. If the engraving is incorrect due to a Northstar Commerce error, the case requires support review.

Agent handling rule: If the item is personalized or customized, ask whether the issue is damage, defect, wrong item, incorrect customization, or not-as-described. Escalate cases involving alleged Northstar Commerce error.

## [POLICY:DIGITAL_PRODUCTS_NONRETURNABLE] Digital Gift Cards And Downloadable Products

Digital gift cards, downloadable products, digital codes, and electronically delivered content cannot be returned, exchanged, or refunded after delivery unless required by law or support confirms a Northstar Commerce fulfillment error.

Citation-friendly rule: Digital gift cards and downloadable products cannot be returned after delivery.

Example: A customer who downloaded a digital guide cannot return it because they no longer need it. A customer who received the wrong digital code should be routed to support review.

Agent handling rule: For digital products, do not offer a standard return path. Escalate only when the customer reports non-delivery, wrong code, duplicate charge, fulfillment error, or a legal compliance concern.

## [POLICY:EXCEPTION_REVIEW_REQUIRED] Exceptions To Restricted Item Rules

Exceptions to final sale, personalized item, digital product, or downloadable product restrictions require human support review. The agent may explain the policy but must not promise an exception, refund, exchange, store credit, or replacement.

Citation-friendly rule: Restricted item exceptions require human support review and cannot be approved automatically by the agent.

Example: A customer asks for an exception because they missed the final sale disclosure. The agent should explain that final sale items are not returnable or exchangeable and route the exception request to support review if the customer wants further consideration.

Agent handling rule: If the customer requests an exception to a restricted-item rule, set escalation required and summarize the policy restriction, customer reason, and any supporting facts.

## [POLICY:RESTRICTED_ITEM_FACTS] Required Facts For Restricted Items

For restricted-item cases, Northstar Commerce support needs the order number, item name, restriction type, customer reason, and whether the item is damaged, defective, incorrect, missing, or not as described.

Citation-friendly rule: Restricted-item review requires order details, restriction type, customer reason, and issue type.

Example: "My custom mug arrived with the wrong name" should collect the order number, photo or description of the incorrect customization, and issue type before support review.

Agent handling rule: Ask for missing restricted-item review facts before escalation when possible, but do not delay escalation if the customer has already provided enough facts to show review is required.
