# Northstar Commerce Escalation Rules

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_POLICY_EXCEPTIONS] Policy Exception Requests

Requests for exceptions to return windows, final sale restrictions, personalized item restrictions, digital product restrictions, inspection outcomes, or shipping-fee rules require human support review. The agent may explain the policy but must not approve an exception.

Citation-friendly rule: Policy exception requests require human support review.

Example: A customer asks to return a final sale item because they did not notice the final sale label. The agent should explain the final sale rule and escalate if the customer wants an exception.

Agent handling rule: Set escalation required when the customer asks for an exception, override, special approval, supervisor decision, or one-time courtesy outside the stated policy.

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_LEGAL_THREATS] Legal Threats And Regulatory Claims

Legal threats, regulatory complaints, chargeback threats, lawsuit references, attorney involvement, or claims that Northstar Commerce violated the law require immediate human support review.

Citation-friendly rule: Legal threats and regulatory claims require immediate human support review.

Example: A customer says they will sue Northstar Commerce unless a refund is issued. The agent should not debate legal issues and should escalate.

Agent handling rule: Keep the response calm and concise. Do not provide legal advice, assess legal merit, or make commitments. Escalate with the exact customer concern summarized neutrally.

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_FRAUD_ABUSE] Fraud Concerns And Repeat Abuse

Fraud concerns, repeat abuse, suspicious return patterns, altered receipts, mismatched order details, unusually frequent missing item claims, or suspected policy manipulation require human support review.

Citation-friendly rule: Fraud concerns and repeat abuse require human support review.

Example: A customer reports missing items on several recent orders or provides order details that do not match the claimed item. Support must review before any refund or replacement decision.

Agent handling rule: Do not accuse the customer of fraud. Escalate neutrally using phrases such as "This requires support review because the order details need verification."

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_AGGRESSIVE_LANGUAGE] Aggressive Or Unsafe Language

Aggressive language, harassment, threats of harm, abusive messages, or unsafe language toward support staff require human review and may require safety handling.

Citation-friendly rule: Aggressive, abusive, threatening, or unsafe language requires human review.

Example: A customer threatens a support employee while demanding a refund. The agent should not continue normal policy negotiation and should escalate.

Agent handling rule: Respond briefly, avoid escalation of tone, and route the case to human review.

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_HIGH_VALUE_REFUNDS] High-Value Refunds Over 500 Dollars

Refund requests over $500 require human support review before approval. High-value cases may require additional identity, order, payment, shipping, and inspection verification.

Citation-friendly rule: Refund requests over $500 require human support review before approval.

Example: A customer requests a $749 refund for an electronics order. Even if the customer provides order details, support review is required before approval.

Agent handling rule: If the customer states or implies the refund value is over $500, set escalation required. If item value is unknown and the product category may be high-value, ask for order value or route to review if other escalation factors are present.

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_MISSING_PROOF_HIGH_VALUE] Missing Proof With High-Value Items

Missing proof of purchase for high-value items requires human support review. The agent must not offer refunds, replacements, exchanges, or store credit for high-value items without proof of purchase.

Citation-friendly rule: Missing proof of purchase with high-value items requires human support review.

Example: A customer asks to return expensive headphones but has no receipt, order number, or gift receipt. Support must review before any outcome is offered.

Agent handling rule: Ask for proof of purchase first. If proof remains unavailable and the item appears high-value, escalate.

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_DAMAGE_RESPONSIBILITY] Unclear Damaged-Item Responsibility

Cases where damaged-item responsibility is unclear require human support review. This includes damage discovered after use, damage reported long after delivery, unclear carrier involvement, unclear customer handling, or conflict between the customer's account and order records.

Citation-friendly rule: Unclear damaged-item responsibility requires human support review.

Example: A customer says a chair broke after several weeks but does not know whether it arrived damaged. Support must review timing, photos, item use, and delivery records before deciding.

Agent handling rule: Escalate and collect delivery date, date issue was noticed, photos or description, package condition, and how the item was used.

## [POLICY:ESCALATION_HANDOFF_SUMMARY] Handoff Summary Requirements

When escalating, the agent should prepare a handoff summary containing customer request, order number if provided, item name, delivery date if known, issue type, requested outcome, known facts, missing facts, relevant policy IDs, and reason for escalation.

Citation-friendly rule: Escalations should include a handoff summary with known facts, missing facts, relevant policy IDs, and escalation reason.

Example: "Customer requests refund for order NC-2002, personalized blanket, wrong embroidered name, photo available. Relevant policies: personalized item exception and support review required."

Agent handling rule: Every escalation response should include a concise customer-facing explanation and a structured handoff summary for support.
