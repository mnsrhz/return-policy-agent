# Northstar Commerce Damaged, Wrong, And Missing Items Policy

## [POLICY:DAMAGE_WRONG_MISSING_REVIEW] Support Review Required

Damaged, defective, missing, wrong item, and not-as-described cases usually require support review before a refund, replacement, exchange, or shipping-fee refund can be approved. Support may need to inspect photos, order records, carrier information, inventory records, and fulfillment notes.

Citation-friendly rule: Damaged, defective, missing, wrong item, and not-as-described cases usually require support review.

Example: A customer reports receiving a cracked vase. The case should be routed to support review because support may need photos and order verification before approving a refund or replacement.

Agent handling rule: Do not mark these cases as automatically eligible for refund or exchange. Set escalation required or route to support review, especially when responsibility is unclear.

## [POLICY:DAMAGE_WRONG_MISSING_REQUIRED_INFO] Required Information For Issue Review

Customers reporting damaged, defective, missing, wrong, or not-as-described items must provide an order number, a photo or clear description of the issue, and the issue type. Issue type should be one of damaged, defective, missing item, wrong item, or not as described.

Citation-friendly rule: Issue review requires order number, photo or description, and issue type.

Example: "My lamp arrived broken" is missing the order number and photo or description details. "Order NC-1007 arrived with a broken lamp base, and I can upload a photo" includes the order number, issue type, and supporting description.

Agent handling rule: If any required information is missing, ask for it. If the customer provides enough information, escalate with a handoff summary.

## [POLICY:DAMAGED_DEFECTIVE_ITEMS] Damaged Or Defective Items

An item is considered damaged or defective when it arrives broken, unusable, materially flawed, or not functioning as intended. Northstar Commerce may request photos, packaging details, serial numbers, or a description of the defect before deciding on refund, replacement, or return shipping reimbursement.

Citation-friendly rule: Damaged or defective items may require photos, packaging details, serial numbers, or defect descriptions before resolution.

Example: A blender that does not power on after delivery may be treated as defective. Support may ask for the order number, description of the defect, and photos or video before approving next steps.

Agent handling rule: Escalate damaged or defective cases after collecting order number and issue evidence. Do not promise replacement inventory or refund approval.

## [POLICY:WRONG_ITEM_RECEIVED] Wrong Item Received

A wrong item case occurs when the customer receives a different product, size, color, quantity, or variant than what appears on the order confirmation. Wrong item cases require order verification and may require a photo of the item received and packing slip.

Citation-friendly rule: Wrong item cases require order verification and may require a photo of the received item and packing slip.

Example: A customer ordered black running shoes in size 9 but received blue running shoes in size 8. Support must verify the order and item received before approving a replacement or return.

Agent handling rule: Ask for the order number, the item ordered, the item received, and a photo or description. Escalate once those facts are available.

## [POLICY:MISSING_ITEMS] Missing Item Claims

A missing item case occurs when an item listed on the order confirmation or packing slip was not included in the delivered package. Missing item cases require order number, item name, package condition, and a description of what was received.

Citation-friendly rule: Missing item claims require order number, item name, package condition, and description of what was received.

Example: A customer ordered three candles but received only two. Support must review the order and fulfillment records before deciding whether to send the missing item or issue a refund.

Agent handling rule: Escalate missing item claims because they may require fulfillment and carrier review. Ask for package condition if the customer did not provide it.

## [POLICY:DAMAGE_RESPONSIBILITY_UNCLEAR] Unclear Responsibility

When it is unclear whether damage was caused by shipping, customer handling, product defect, carrier handling, or normal wear after delivery, the case requires human review. The agent must not assign responsibility.

Citation-friendly rule: Unclear damaged-item responsibility requires human review.

Example: A customer says a table "broke after two weeks" but does not explain whether it arrived damaged or broke during use. Support review is required before deciding responsibility.

Agent handling rule: Escalate unclear responsibility cases and ask for delivery date, when the issue was first noticed, item condition on arrival, photos, and description of use.
